<h2>Modelling the paper-drying process</h2>
<p>Exploring how models of a real industrial process can help estimate energy consumption across different scenarios.</p>
<h3>The question</h3><p>How can we represent the drying section of a paper manufacturing process, and compare the energy needs of different configurations?</p>
<h3>What I worked on</h3>
<ul><li>Built on the existing Modelica library DryLib to model the drying section using an object-oriented approach, including environmental parameters.</li><li>Exported FMUs for six configurations, based on use cases specified by partner companies.</li><li>Ran simulations with the CVODE solver using OMSimulator in Python and FMI.jl in Julia.</li></ul>
