---
title: Maple-roasted pumpkin with chilli & feta
date: 2026-10-27
summary: Roasted pumpkin with maple syrup, walnuts and red onion, finished with feta, fresh chilli and parsley.
serves: "3 as a main, or 6 as a side"
prep_minutes: 10
cook_minutes: 30
measurement_note: "Converted weights are approximate. Cup measures use a 240–250 ml cup and tablespoons use 15 ml. Weigh the pumpkin before peeling; the prepared amount will be smaller. Salt weight varies with crystal size, so adjust to taste."
ingredients:
  - "1,000 g whole pumpkin, weighed before peeling; peel, deseed and cut into 2.5 cm cubes"
  - "1 red onion, peeled and cut into 1 cm wedges"
  - "60 g walnuts, roughly chopped"
  - "40 g olive oil"
  - "60 g maple syrup, or approximately 65 g honey"
  - "Approximately 4 g cooking salt; use less initially and adjust for your salt and taste"
  - "2–3 g dried chilli flakes, adjusted to taste, or omit"
  - "Optional finish: 110 g Danish or Greek feta, crumbled"
  - "Optional finish: 1 large red chilli, deseeded and finely chopped"
  - "Optional finish: approximately 4 g parsley, finely chopped"
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200°C conventional or 180°C fan. Line a large roasting tray with baking paper."
  - "Put the pumpkin cubes, onion wedges and walnuts on the tray. Add the olive oil and maple syrup, then turn everything until evenly coated. Add the salt and dried chilli flakes and mix again."
  - "Spread the mixture out with space between the pumpkin pieces. Use another tray if needed: a crowded tray traps steam and prevents the edges from roasting."
  - "Roast for about 30 minutes, turning the mixture after 20 minutes. The pumpkin should be tender with lightly coloured edges. If the onion is browning too quickly, tuck it underneath the pumpkin for the remaining time."
  - "Gently turn the cooked mixture through the juices on the tray. Arrange on a platter, or transfer to a serving bowl. Scatter over the feta, fresh chilli and parsley, if using, and serve straight away."
source_tips:
  - "Use an eating pumpkin, butternut squash or sweet potato rather than a pumpkin grown for carving."
  - "Other large nuts can replace the walnuts. Add small nuts or seeds, such as pine nuts, sunflower seeds or pumpkin seeds, halfway through roasting instead of at the start."
  - "Aim for tender pumpkin with light caramelisation rather than a deeply browned surface; the maple-coated edges can burn before the whole cube turns golden."
  - "For a mixed, rustic finish, add most of the garnishes on the tray, transfer to a bowl, and add the rest on top."
  - "The original recipe gives leftovers a refrigerator life of up to 3 days."
