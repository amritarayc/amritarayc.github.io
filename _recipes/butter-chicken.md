---
title: Butter chicken
date: 2026-10-28
summary: Pan-fried chicken in a smooth tomato, cashew and butter gravy, finished with cream and kasoori methi.
serves: "4–5"
measurement_note: "Spoon-to-gram conversions are approximate and assume a 15 ml tablespoon and 5 ml teaspoon. Paste consistency, spice grind and how tightly ingredients are packed affect the weight. Oil, water and salt were not quantified in the original; add as needed."
ingredient_groups:
  - name: For the chicken
    items:
      - "300 g boneless chicken breast, cut into bite-sized pieces"
      - "15 g ginger–garlic paste"
      - "8 g red chilli powder"
      - "Salt, to taste"
      - "Oil, as needed for pan-frying"
  - name: For the gravy
    items:
      - "500 g tomatoes, roughly chopped"
      - "100 g onions, roughly chopped"
      - "15 g garlic paste"
      - "50 g cashews"
      - "1 g kasoori methi (dried fenugreek leaves), plus a little extra to garnish"
      - "1 g garam masala"
      - "50 g sugar"
      - "16 g Kashmiri chilli powder"
      - "70 g butter, divided"
      - "45 g cream, plus a little extra to garnish"
      - "30 g malt vinegar, or approximately 23 g white vinegar"
      - "Salt, to taste"
      - "Oil, as needed for cooking the onions"
      - "Water, as needed to simmer the tomatoes and cashews"
steps:
  - "Mix the chicken with the ginger–garlic paste, red chilli powder and salt. Cover and leave to marinate in the fridge for 15–20 minutes."
  - "Heat enough oil to pan-fry the chicken. Cook the pieces through, then transfer them to a bowl. The centre of the thickest piece should reach 74°C."
  - "In the same pan, add the onions, a little oil and about 14 g of the measured butter. Cook until the onions soften, then add the tomatoes and cashews."
  - "Add the garlic paste, salt, vinegar, sugar, garam masala and Kashmiri chilli powder. Pour in enough water to allow the mixture to simmer without catching, stir, and simmer for 15–20 minutes."
  - "Let the mixture cool enough to blend safely, then blend until very smooth. Pass it through a sieve back into the pan, pressing the mixture through to retain as much sauce as possible."
  - "Stir in the remaining butter, the cream, cooked chicken and kasoori methi. Simmer gently for 5–7 minutes, until the chicken is hot and the sauce is combined."
  - "Finish with a little extra cream and kasoori methi, then serve."
