---
title: Pasta al forno saporita
date: 2026-10-28
summary: A hearty baked pasta with sausage, tomato and béchamel sauce, mozzarella, and a golden cheese topping.
serves: 4
measurement_note: "The original calls for 200 ml béchamel; approximately 200 g is used here, with the exact weight depending on the sauce’s thickness. Oil and salt are added as needed."
ingredients:
  - "350 g mezzi rigatoni, or another short pasta shape"
  - "200 g Italian sausages, casings removed"
  - "500 g tomato passata"
  - "Approximately 200 g béchamel sauce"
  - "250 g mozzarella, diced and divided"
  - "40 g grated cheese, divided"
  - "½ onion, finely chopped"
  - "Extra-virgin olive oil, as needed"
  - "Fine salt, to taste"
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 180°C fan or 190°C conventional. Bring a large pot of water to the boil for the pasta."
  - "Heat a little olive oil in a frying pan and gently fry the chopped onion until softened."
  - "Crumble the sausages into the pan and fry until browned, breaking them into small pieces as they cook."
  - "Stir in the passata and season with salt. Simmer for about 10 minutes, stirring occasionally."
  - "Meanwhile, salt the boiling water and add the pasta. Cook for half the time stated on the packet; it will finish cooking in the oven."
  - "Take the sauce off the heat and stir in the béchamel until evenly combined. Drain the half-cooked pasta and mix it into the sauce."
  - "Set aside a little mozzarella and grated cheese for the topping. Stir the rest into the pasta, then transfer everything to a baking dish."
  - "Scatter the reserved mozzarella and grated cheese over the top. Bake for about 25 minutes, until the pasta is cooked and the topping is golden."
  - "Serve while hot and the cheese is still melted."
source_tips:
  - "Other short pasta shapes that hold sauce well, such as penne, mezze maniche or tortiglioni, also work."
  - "The source suggests pancetta, speck or guanciale as alternatives to sausage."
  - "For a meat-free variation, the source suggests replacing the sausage with vegetables, extra cheese or a plant-based alternative."
  - "Cooking cream, loosened with a little milk if needed, can replace the béchamel. If using tomato sauce alone, increase the amount so there is enough moisture for the pasta to finish cooking."
  - "Store leftovers covered in the refrigerator for up to 2 days. Reheat thoroughly in the microwave, oven or air fryer."
